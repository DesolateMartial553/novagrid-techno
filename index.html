About Us

An up-and-coming studio, built on real results

NovaGrid Techno started with a simple observation: a lot of good local businesses in and around Birmingham still don't have a website worth having. We set out to fix that — building full, working sites first, so owners could see exactly what a proper web presence looks like before committing to anything.

That approach became our whole method. We design every site from scratch around the business it's for, write copy that actually reflects how that business talks about itself, and ship it live rather than leaving it as a mockup. Our growing portfolio of live client work speaks for itself.

Our approach

No page builders. No recycled templates. Every project gets its own layout, its own copy, and gets tested live in a real browser before we call it done. We build small, and we build it right.

Good To Know

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost? +
It depends on scope — every project is priced individually rather than off a fixed menu. Message us with what you need and we'll give you a straight answer.
How long does a project take? +
Most small business sites go from first conversation to a live URL within one to two weeks, depending on how much content you have ready.
Do you only work with Birmingham businesses? +
Birmingham is where we started, but we work with small businesses anywhere — everything is built and delivered remotely.
Can you update the site after it's live? +
Yes — ongoing updates are part of what we offer. Most content or copy changes go live within minutes of a request.
Do you build the site before I commit to anything? +
Often, yes. We'd rather show you a real, working concept of your site than ask you to commit based on a pitch alone.
